# Research
## Integrated optical beam steering for sensing and communication
Optical beam steering enables optical detection and imaging in macroscopic or microscopic scales and long-range communication over free space. It underpins numerous optical applications, including LiDAR, biomedical imaging, and remote sensing. Despite the inherent speed of light, advanced applications increasingly require the ability to steer multiple beams simultaneously to increase imaging throughput, boost communication bandwidth, and control arrays qubits for scalable quantum computing. We are developing integrated acousto-optic beam steering (AOBS) systems with photonic integrated circuits to enable a scalable platform for multi-beam steering, potentially advancing many areas of optical technologies and enabling novel applications.

Ref: Li, et al., Nature (2023)
